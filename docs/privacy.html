NextDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026
App package: com.rizaaksu.nextday
Contact: via Google Play developer listing for NextDay

NextDay (“the App”) helps users track habit streaks and daily motivation. This policy explains what data we process and why.

1. Data stored on your device

By default, NextDay stores data locally on your device, including:

  • Anonymous local profile (optional display name, age, gender)
  • Selected habit interests and streak start dates
  • Daily milestone / motivation entries
  • App settings (language, notifications, ads-removed purchase flag)

This data stays on your device unless you export a backup yourself. Uninstalling the App may delete local data.

2. Account

NextDay creates a local anonymous profile automatically. No email/password account is required for core use in the current version.

3. Advertising

The free version may show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and ad interaction data under Google’s policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase (“Remove ads”).

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS). We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase status may be stored locally to unlock ad-free access.

5. Optional AI features

If AI motivation is enabled, limited context (habit type, day number, optional profile fields, recent local notes) may be sent to an AI provider or proxy to generate text. Do not enter sensitive medical information you do not want processed.

6. Notifications

With your permission, the App may schedule local reminder notifications on your device. These are generated locally in the current version.

7. Children

NextDay is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

8. Health disclaimer

NextDay provides general information and motivational content only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

9. Your choices

  • Change language and notification settings in the App
  • Purchase Remove ads
  • Export a local JSON backup from Account
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

11. Contact

Use the contact email shown on the NextDay Google Play listing.
