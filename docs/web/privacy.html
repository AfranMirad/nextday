NextDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026
Developer: NextDay / Riza Aksu
Contact: support@nextday.app

NextDay (“the App”) helps users track habit streaks and daily motivation. This policy explains what data we process and why.

1. Data we store on your device

By default, NextDay stores data locally on your device, including:

  • Anonymous local profile (optional display name, age, gender)
  • Selected habit interests and streak start dates
  • Daily milestone / motivation entries
  • App settings (language, notifications, ads-removed purchase flag)

This data stays on your device unless you export a backup file yourself. Uninstalling the App may delete local data.

2. Account

NextDay creates a local anonymous profile automatically. No email/password account is required for core use in the current version.

3. Advertising

If you use the free version, the App may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and approximate device/ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, subject to Google’s policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase (“Remove ads”).

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS). We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase status may be stored locally to unlock ad-free access.

5. Optional AI features

If AI motivation is enabled, limited context (habit type, day number, optional profile fields, recent local notes) may be sent to an AI provider or our proxy to generate text. Do not enter sensitive medical information you do not want processed.

6. Notifications

With your permission, the App may schedule local reminder notifications on your device. These are generated locally and are not sent to our servers in the current version.

7. Children

NextDay is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the App if you are under the applicable age.

8. Health disclaimer

NextDay provides general information and motivational content only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Seek professional help for addiction or health concerns.

9. Your choices

  • Change language and notification settings in the App
  • Purchase Remove ads to stop showing ads in the App
  • Export a local JSON backup from Account
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data from the device

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions: support@nextday.app
